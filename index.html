How a native speaker would actually write it

Each step turns one English word and all its repetitions into Greek, in English word order so the swap is a clear vocabulary match. The moment a whole sentence is Greek it snaps into natural Greek syntax — words reorder, articles contract (σε + το → στο), and pronouns drop (a dotted underline marks a reflowed sentence).
